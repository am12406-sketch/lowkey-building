Redesign the entire app with the following changes. This is a full visual and copy overhaul — do not change any core functionality, just the design, copy, and a few small UX additions.

TYPOGRAPHY
Use Cormorant Garamond (import from Google Fonts) for all headlines, quest titles, and reflection text. Use DM Sans for all UI labels, buttons, navigation, and small text. These two fonts together are the entire identity of the app.

Borders: #EDEAE2

Dark surfaces (suggestion card, done screen): #1A1A1A with #F5F2EC text
Skill colors:
  Social: #E8685A
  Health: #4CAF7D
  Mind: #5B8DEF
  Career: #F5A623
