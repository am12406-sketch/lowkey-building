Redesign the entire UI/UX to feel playful, energetic, and slightly chaotic in a fun way. 

This app should feel like a colorful life RPG game, not a SaaS dashboard or productivity tool.

PRIMARY EMOTION: Excitement.

VISUAL DIRECTION:
- Remove all corporate/dashboard styling.
- No dark navy or muted tones.
- Use bold, vibrant accent colors.
- Light, bright background (off-white or very light lavender).
- Add subtle floating gradient blobs or playful background shapes.
- Large rounded corners everywhere (rounded-3xl or more).
- Thick, colorful XP bars.
- Bigger icons with slight bounce animation.
- Use a playful modern font like Poppins, Outfit, or Baloo 2.
- Increase spacing and reduce text density.

UX STRUCTURE CHANGES:

1. Make “Today’s Adventure” the hero section at the top.
   - Big playful heading.
   - 3 quest cards displayed prominently.
   - Each quest should feel like a collectible card.
   - Slight tilt or hover lift animation.
   - When selected, card scales slightly and others fade subtly.

2. Move stats below adventure.
   Rename “Your Skills” to “Your Character Build”.
   Make skill cards feel like power meters, not analytics widgets.

3. Life Level should be a big circular badge.
   - Add radial gradient glow.
   - Slight floating animation.
   - Make the level title bold and fun (e.g., WANDERER).

4. Completion Experience:
   - When “Claim XP” is pressed, animate XP bar dramatically.
   - Add playful confetti burst.
   - Slight screen shake or bounce effect.
   - Show large "+XP" text briefly.
   - If level up, glow animation on badge.

5. Reduce analytical language.
   Avoid phrases like:
   - Total XP
   - Remaining XP
   - Metrics-heavy wording

   Keep it simple and energetic.

6. Add micro-surprise moments.
   Occasionally show:
   - “✨ Bonus XP!”
   - Sparkle animation
   - Random fun encouragement message

OVERALL FEEL:
This should feel like an indie game UI with slight chaos and personality.
It should feel alive, colorful, and playful.
Not minimal SaaS.
Not corporate.
Not productivity software.

Make it feel like you are playing your life, not managing it.
